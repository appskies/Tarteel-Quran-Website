Discover Your Path

Quran reading, prayer times and daily dhikr in one app

🕐

Prayer Times & Reminders

Check prayer times for your city, with the Hijri date and a countdown to the next prayer time. Turn on optional reminders for prayer times, subject to your device's notification settings. Reminders use the default notification sound and do not play an adhan. The Qibla compass uses your location and your phone's compass.

✪

Dua Collection & Reading Goals

Browse duas for everyday situations, including Morning & Evening, with Arabic, transliteration and meaning. Set daily reading goals by pages, juz or minutes and keep your reading streak going.

🙏

Tasbih Counter & Quran Reader

Keep count of your dhikr with a digital Tasbih that remembers your count, and explore the 99 Names of Allah with their meanings. Read all 114 surahs in Arabic with a translation alongside.

AI Quran Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription. It includes the AI assistant, three more reciters and surah downloads. AI answers can be inaccurate and are general information, not a religious ruling (fatwa) or a substitute for a qualified scholar. Check important matters with a trusted source.

Why Choose AI Quran?

A simple companion for your daily Quran reading

🌟

Listen to Recitation

Listen ayah by ayah, and repeat an ayah or a whole surah. Premium adds three more reciters and surah downloads for offline listening. Audio streams over the internet.

📖

Quran Text Built In

The Arabic text and translations are included in the app, so reading keeps working if your connection drops. AI Quran needs an internet connection to start, and audio and the AI assistant need one too.

🌐

Translations in Many Languages

Read a translation beside the Arabic text. It follows your device language, and English is used when a language is not available. The app itself comes in 34 languages.
